Soft Spaces / CML
v42.1 — DATASET DISCOVERY + ELIGIBILITY AUDIT

STATUS
------
PRE-MODELING DATASET AUDIT

No CML expression matrix is modeled in this step.

PURPOSE
-------
Identify a biologically meaningful primary CML phenotype and a development /
external-replication cohort pair suitable for the v42 Soft-Spaces protocol.

PRIMARY TARGET PREFERENCE
-------------------------
The preferred v42 target remains treatment response / molecular response rather
than trivial CML-vs-normal classification.

CANDIDATE DATASETS
------------------

1. GSE130404
   Title:
   Gene expression signature that predicts early molecular response failure
   in chronic phase CML patients on frontline imatinib.

   Human: YES
   Disease: chronic-phase CML
   Reported cohort: 96 patients at diagnosis
   Primary phenotype candidate:
       early molecular response (EMR) failure,
       BCR-ABL1 >10% IS at 3 months
   Strengths:
       - clinically meaningful endpoint
       - diagnosis-time expression
       - larger modern response-prediction cohort
       - direct fit with v42.0 phenotype preference
   Risks / audit items:
       - verify exact platform and processed matrix
       - verify class counts
       - verify independent validation samples / whether they are deposited
       - ensure outcome labels can be frozen before modeling

2. GSE44589
   Title:
   Gene expression-based imatinib response prediction in chronic phase CML.

   Human: YES
   Disease: CML
   Reported design:
       135 patient samples before imatinib
       63 samples at 6 weeks after starting imatinib
   Primary phenotype candidate:
       imatinib response
   Strengths:
       - substantial patient count
       - direct treatment-response framing
       - pretreatment expression available
   Risks / audit items:
       - distinguish unique patients from repeated time points
       - freeze pretreatment-only analysis if used for prediction
       - resolve response labels unambiguously
       - check overlap with any other published cohorts

3. GSE2535
   Title:
   Expression data in patients with chronic myelogenous leukemia for response
   to imatinib.

   Human: YES
   Disease: CML
   Explicit design:
       training set: Leipzig, 8 responders + 5 non-responders
       validation set: Mannheim, 8 responders + 7 non-responders
   Platform:
       Affymetrix HG-U95Av2
   Strengths:
       - explicit independent training/validation split
       - clean response phenotype
       - historically useful replication dataset
   Risks:
       - very small sample size
       - unsuitable as main development cohort
   Proposed role:
       possible external/secondary replication only.

4. GSE14671
   Title:
   Expression signature to predict major cytogenetic response in chronic phase
   CML patients treated with imatinib.

   Human: YES
   Disease: chronic-phase CML
   Reported training set:
       N=36 CD34+ samples
   Phenotype:
       major cytogenetic response / cytogenetic resistance after 12 months
   Strengths:
       - clinically meaningful response endpoint
       - purified CD34+ population
   Risks:
       - modest sample size
       - cellular composition differs from bulk blood cohorts
       - must verify external validation availability

5. GSE236233
   Title:
   Single cell multi-omics analysis of chronic myeloid leukemia links cellular
   heterogeneity to therapy response.

   Human: YES
   Data type:
       CITE-seq / single-cell multi-omics
   Phenotype:
       molecular response after 12 months of TKI
   Strengths:
       - biologically highly relevant
       - directly addresses persistence / stem-progenitor heterogeneity
   Risks:
       - single-cell data require a distinct preprocessing and statistical
         protocol
       - pseudoreplication risk
       - should not be mixed casually with bulk-array v42 development
   Proposed role:
       future v43/single-cell branch, not initial v42 bulk-expression track.

EXCLUDED AS PRIMARY HUMAN RESPONSE COHORTS
------------------------------------------
GSE120932:
    K562 / K562-IR cell lines, not patient cohort.

GSE24493:
    CML cell lines treated with imatinib, not patient cohort.

GSE43754:
    5 CML + 5 healthy donors, stem/progenitor biology rather than TKI-response;
    too small for primary response modeling.

GSE33075 / GSE12211:
    valuable paired treatment-effect studies, but designed primarily around
    within-patient expression change rather than baseline response prediction.

IMPORTANT
---------
This shortlist does NOT authorize modeling yet.

Before v42.2, we must verify for the preferred pair:

- exact platform(s)
- actual downloadable sample count
- response-label definition
- class balance
- unique-patient status
- pre-treatment vs post-treatment samples
- probe/gene mapping quality
- whether external cohort is genuinely independent
- whether all preprocessing can be frozen without inspecting external outcomes

PROVISIONAL PRIMARY BIOLOGICAL QUESTION
---------------------------------------
Can a Soft-Spaces-derived baseline expression subspace identify chronic-phase
CML patients at increased risk of inadequate molecular response to frontline
TKI treatment, and does that subspace transfer to an independent response
cohort better than matched NULL?

This question remains provisional until the dataset eligibility audit is
completed.

STOP RULES
----------
Do not proceed to v42.2 if:

- response labels are ambiguous;
- classes are too small for defensible resampling;
- development and replication cohorts overlap materially;
- only post-treatment expression is available for a prediction claim;
- no independent external cohort can support the frozen representation.
