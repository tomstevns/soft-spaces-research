=== DECISION ===
The positive interference/coherence direction survives all three term counts.
C_group is especially robust: positive seedwise correlation in
  7 terms: dephasing 11/12, transverse 11/12
  9 terms: dephasing 11/12, transverse 8/12
 11 terms: dephasing 12/12, transverse 12/12

A_group also retains a positive median seedwise correlation for every
tested term count and both perturbation families.

Conclusion:
  The degeneracy-closed interference/coherence mechanism is not confined
  to the frozen 11-term Hamiltonian. It survives moderate model variation.
  The 9-term transverse case is the weakest robustness point and should be
  treated as a useful stress case rather than hidden.
