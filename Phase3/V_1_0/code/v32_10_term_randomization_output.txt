=== DECISION GUIDE ===
Support for a mechanistic coherence role requires BOTH:
  A) observed C_E systematically differs from the norm-preserving randomized null,
  B) coherence excess/z retains a positive cross-seed association with prominence.

If A and B hold, the hotspot signal depends on alignment/cancellation
between virtual Q pathways, not merely on their individual magnitudes.
This is stronger than correlation, but still a computational intervention
rather than a complete analytic derivation.
